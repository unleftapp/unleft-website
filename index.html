PRIVATE LIFE ADMIN

Turn saved things into handled things.

Unleft helps you turn photos, screenshots, notes, bills, returns, appointments, and voice thoughts into private action cards you can review, remind, and complete.

Private by design. Built for calm. Made for the things you meant to come back to.

Most saved things are not forgotten. They are postponed.

They sit in Photos, Messages, Notes, email, and your mind. Not because they are unimportant. Because life gets busy. Unleft gives those unfinished things a calm place to become action cards, reminders, and completed actions.

WHY UNLEFT EXISTS

Saved should not mean forgotten.

Unleft exists for the things people intend to come back to: screenshots, appointments, return labels, bills, notes, ideas, and reminders.

The goal is not to become another task manager.

The goal is to reduce mental clutter and help important things get handled.

PRIVACY FIRST

You stay in control.

Nothing becomes a card unless you approve it.

Permissions are requested only when needed.

You decide what is saved, reviewed, reminded, completed, exported, and deleted.

Unleft is designed around ownership, visibility, and user control rather than data collection.

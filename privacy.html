PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy is part of the product.

Last updated: June 2026

Unleft is built for private life admin. The things people save can be personal: bills, appointments, return labels, reminders, screenshots, notes, and voice thoughts. We treat that privacy as a core product requirement, not an afterthought.

What you choose to save

Unleft may store the cards, titles, actions, reminder times, notes, photos, screenshots, camera captures, or voice notes that you choose to add to the app.

Control and approval

Nothing becomes a card unless you choose to create or approve it. You remain responsible for reviewing card details, reminder times, and actions before relying on them.

Permissions

Unleft may request access to Photos, Camera, Microphone, and Notifications only when those features are needed. You can manage these permissions through your iPhone settings.

Reminders and notifications

If you set a reminder, Unleft may schedule a notification on your device. Notifications are intended as helpful prompts, not guarantees.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not build Unleft around advertising profiles or data resale.

Deleting data

You can delete cards inside the app. If your version includes a delete-all option, you can also clear saved cards from the app controls.

Early beta note

Unleft is currently in early testing. Features, storage behavior, and privacy controls may improve over time as the product develops.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello.unleft@gmail.com

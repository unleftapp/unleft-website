HOW UNLEFT WORKS

Start with the thing—not a blank task.

Unleft keeps the original source, the next action, and the completion state connected from beginning to end.

01
CAPTURE

Choose something real.

Select a screenshot or photo, take a picture, share an image into Unleft, record a voice thought, or create a card manually.

  • Homework sheet or assignment screenshot
  • Appointment card or message
  • Bill, receipt, form, or return label
  • Idea or follow-up spoken aloud
PHOTOSchool formSigned copy due Friday
VOICECall the officeAsk about the new date
02
REVIEW

You approve every detail.

On-device text recognition can suggest a title, next action, or date from the image you selected. Nothing is saved automatically—you review and edit first.

03
PLACE

Open when actionable. Saved when useful.

Open is for things that still need a next step. Saved is for quiet reference. This separation keeps urgency meaningful.

OPENNeeds action
SAVEDKeep for reference
04
REMIND

Add timing only when it helps.

Choose a reminder for an Open card. Saved items remain quiet until you decide they need attention.

ReminderTomorrow · 9:00 AM“Submit the signed school form.”
05
HANDLE

Close the loop.

Mark the card Handled when the action is complete. Reopen it later if life changes. The source and history remain understandable.

✓
HandledOne less unfinished thing.
A CALM DEFAULT

The app works without an account.

Keep everything on the device, or sign in and explicitly enable optional backup. The choice stays visible and reversible.

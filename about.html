ABOUT UNLEFT

Private life admin, kept simple.

Unleft was built for the things people save because they matter, but later forget to handle.

Why we built it

Most people already have a system, but it is scattered across Photos, Messages, Notes, email, memory, and screenshots. Unleft gives those saved things a calm place to become cards, reminders, and completed actions.

What Unleft is

Unleft is a private action-card system. It helps you capture something, review it, set a reminder, and mark it handled.

What Unleft is not

Unleft is not a noisy task manager, social app, or AI hype product. It is designed to stay focused on helping saved things become handled things.

Our promise

Nothing becomes a card unless you approve it. You stay in control from capture to completion.

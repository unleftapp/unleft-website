ABOUT UNLEFT

Private life admin, kept simple.

Unleft was built for the meaningful things people save because they matter—and then quietly fail to return to.

THE INSIGHT

The problem is rarely capture. It is follow-through.

People already capture constantly: screenshots, photos, voice notes, messages, receipts, documents, and reminders to themselves.

The missing layer is a calm way to decide what each source means, what happens next, and when the loop is complete.

PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

What Unleft is designed to protect.

01

User approval

Nothing becomes a card without review. Suggestions remain suggestions.

02

Useful simplicity

The product stays focused on Capture, Review, Open or Saved, Reminder, and Handled.

03

Source context

The original photo, screenshot, or voice note remains connected to the next action.

04

Privacy by choice

Local-first use remains available. Account and backup features are optional.

05

Closure

Handled is not just a checkmark. It is a visible ending to an unfinished loop.

06

Honest claims

No false AI magic, no hidden uploads, and no security language beyond what the product actually provides.

THE MISSION

Help ordinary people carry less unfinished mental weight.

Unleft is being built deliberately: reliability before hype, privacy before growth tricks, and usefulness before feature count.

Saved should not mean forgotten.

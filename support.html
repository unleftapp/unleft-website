SUPPORT

Need help with Unleft?

Tell us what happened without sharing more personal information than necessary.

Email support

Email hello@unleft.app. Include your iPhone model, app version and build number, what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and a screenshot when useful.

Protect your information

Never email passwords, six-digit sign-in codes, private Apple credentials, full financial documents, or sensitive source files. Support may ask for a safer, limited description instead.

Family beta feedback

During beta testing, report confusing wording, missing files, reminder problems, slow interactions, layout issues, accessibility barriers, backup or restore concerns, and anything that makes the Capture → Review → Handled loop harder.

Useful subject lines

  • Unleft Bug Report
  • Unleft Backup or Restore
  • Unleft Account Help
  • Unleft Accessibility Feedback
  • Unleft Early Access

Response expectations

Support is currently founder-led. We aim to review meaningful beta and account issues promptly, but response times are not guaranteed.

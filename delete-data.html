DELETE DATA

You remain in control.

Use the in-app controls to delete cards, local data, or an optional cloud account.

Deletion is permanent. Export anything you need before confirming a destructive action.

Delete an individual card

Open the card detail screen and choose Delete Card. The card is removed from local storage, and its managed local attachment is removed when it is no longer referenced.

Delete all local cards

Open the Privacy & Data controls inside Unleft and choose the delete-all option. Review the confirmation carefully. This removes local cards, managed attachments, and scheduled reminders from the current device.

Delete your cloud account

Open Control Center → Account & Sync → Delete Account. Follow both confirmation steps. The in-app process is designed to delete cloud cards, attachment metadata, private cloud files, profile data, and the authentication account. The confirmation explains whether local cards remain on the current iPhone.

Sign in with Apple

For accounts created with Sign in with Apple, Unleft is completing the required server-side Apple authorization revocation step before public App Store release.

Need help?

Email hello@unleft.app with the subject “Delete My Unleft Data.” Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.
